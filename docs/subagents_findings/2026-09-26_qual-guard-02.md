# QUAL-GUARD-01 R3: qualifier coverage is clause-scoped (dms#290)

Keywords: QUAL-GUARD-01, unhonored_qualifier, coverage, GROUP BY, WHERE, word boundary, time_filter, dms-290
Main idea: The merged guard (3f0353a6) matched text. The Epic stamp named what it could not prove. Coverage now reads the clause a qualifier must live in: grain and dimension in GROUP BY (ordinals resolved through SELECT) or plan `group_by`; named filter and year/month in WHERE/HAVING or plan `filters`; "last N <unit>" needs a clock-or-date-anchored predicate windowed in that unit. Word-bounded, paren-aware.

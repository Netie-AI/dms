## What

Chat (`AnswerMessage`) and Studio (`ChatTurn`) render a clarify card or an access card only when the envelope parses. `clarify` needs `clarify_id`, a question, and 1 to 3 readings. `access_needed` needs `table` and at least one `{id, name}`. `can_request` is true only for boolean true. A tap calls `postClarifyPick`, which adds `clarify_id` and `reading_id` to `POST /v1/chat/ask`. Without both ids the ask JSON is unchanged. Switch sets the Space and re-asks that question. Ask the steward is `POST /v1/spaces/{id}/access-requests`. A 404, 405, or 501 sets the route missing and the button unmounts. The response body is not shown.

## Not a word rule

Parsers check field types. The button label is the reading label from the envelope. No question-text table.

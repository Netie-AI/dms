## What

`DMS_SIGN` is off unless the value is the digit 1. Off, the router is not mounted. On, pyHanko writes a PAdES signature. The tenant key is OpenVault `signkey/{tenant}` and `signcert/{tenant}`. Tests use a self-signed cert. Typed, drawn, and uploaded captures require the sentence "I agree to sign electronically". A model actor cannot sign. Sequential and parallel signers use expiring one-time links. Disclose can watermark, redact named fields into a new PDF, and revoke. Verify checks the signature and the hash.

## Hash chain vs the product ledger

`sign_audit_events.prev_hash` and `event_hash` are the e-sign evidence certificate. `ledger_ref` has no `prev_hash`. `DMS_SIGN_LEDGER` must be the digit 1 before a Cortex pointer is written. The payload has no key, token, or image.

## Ceiling

ponytail: `_PROCESS_LOCK` signs one document at a time in a process. Postgres also takes an advisory transaction lock and rechecks the tip. Upgrade: a per-document lock if more than one worker must sign the same document at once. Redaction builds a new PDF from the remaining fields. It does not rewrite the signed original. A qualified signature still needs a licensed CA. This cert is not one.
